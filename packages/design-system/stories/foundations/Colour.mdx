# Colour

Two layers. **Layer 1** is eight Radix-structured scales that no component may
consume directly. **Layer 2** is the semantic aliases, which are the API.

Every swatch below is **probed out of the DOM**, not transcribed. Each token is
painted onto a hidden element inside a real `[data-theme]` subtree and read
back with `getComputedStyle`, so what you see is what the browser resolved —
through `light-dark()` and through however many `var()` hops the chain takes.
A hex typed into this page could be wrong; a probed one cannot.

## Layer 1 — raw palette

Steps 1–8 are curve-placed. Steps 9 and 10 are anchored on the Figma brand
values. Steps 11, 12, `edge` and `press` are **contrast-solved** against every
surface a foreground can legally land on, including other scales' tinted fills
— not eyeballed. See [Contrast](?path=/docs/foundations-contrast--docs) for the
measurements.

## Layer 2 — semantic aliases

The only colour tokens a component may reference. Where light and dark point at
different raw steps, that is deliberate: `--surface-raised` is lighter than the
page in *both* themes, which takes `sand-1` in light and `sand-4` in dark.

The **resolves to** column comes from the token JSON. A blank there means the
alias exists only in the CSS — see `TOKEN-DRIFT.md`.
