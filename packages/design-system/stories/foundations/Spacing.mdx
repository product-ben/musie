# Spacing & targets

Base unit **4px**. `sp-1`–`sp-8` reproduce the Figma Spacing collection exactly;
`sp-9`/`sp-10` are additions for desktop section rhythm.

The semantic steps carry the rule that makes the app feel light: **the jump
between groups is always at least two steps larger than the gap inside one.**

## Touch targets — WCAG 2.5.8

`--target-min` is the absolute floor and carries a condition: 24px is legal for
inline controls in prose and card controls on a *fine* pointer, and never for a
primary action.
