# Contrast

Every documented foreground/background pair, **measured in your browser right
now**.

No number on this page is transcribed. Each pair's two colours are probed out
of a real `[data-theme]` subtree, then run through WCAG 2.2 relative luminance
and `(L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05)`. The audited figures in
`docs/04-contrast-audit.md` are deliberately not printed — a copied ratio is a
claim about a palette that may since have moved.

The **drift** column is the one place the stored audit is read: it compares the
live ratio against the figure recorded when `tokens/_audit.json` was generated.
Anything other than `—` means the palette has changed underneath the audit.

`req` of `—` marks a pair documented as **exempt**: disabled text is outside
1.4.3, so it is measured and reported but never failed.
