# Instrument shadows

The harp's multi-layer shadow, shared by every instrument — and the colours it
falls from. Token gap G5 in `musy-foundations-amendments.css`; the arithmetic
is `src/instrumentShadow.ts`.

A canvas cannot take a `box-shadow`, so these tokens are the shadow's
**parameters**: level, oomph, crispy, resolution, the light's position and
whether the shadow is tinted from the surface. `instrumentShadowLayers()`
turns them into layers; `instrumentShadowProfile()` into the cross-section
each layer paints along a string or a bar. After Josh Comeau's shadow palette
generator, with the values the harp shipped on 4 November.

## Colour by family

A string instrument is lighter than a key instrument. The harp is a string;
the marimba and the kalimba are keys.
